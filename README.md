# MegaStream: The Cancel Screen (Boss Fight)

Keytos Hack · Mini-challenge 01 · *Worst Cancel Screen Ever*

A made-up streaming service with the most user-hostile cancellation flow I could design. Every screen breaks a real usability rule on purpose. It is hostile, not broken: a determined user can always cancel.

**Play:** open `index.html` in a browser. No build, no dependencies. Press **J** to turn off the session-timeout reset (for judges).

## The boss fight
It opens with a boss-encounter title screen: *"Level 99 Retention Specialist. It has never been defeated. PRESS START."* The cancel screen has an HP bar, and each screen you survive knocks one bar off.

1. **Lights off + confirmshaming.** The page is pitch black except for a flashlight around your cursor; only the guilt text and the KEEP button are lit, and the exit is hidden in the dark. The headline rewrites itself every time you go for the exit. Also: a guilt-trip headline, giant green KEEP button that grows when you approach the exit, a pre-ticked Platinum upgrade that re-ticks itself, a fake countdown, a "Don't not keep" double negative, and a nearly invisible real exit.
2. **Retention offers.** Every cancellation reason leads to an offer. You must decline three.
3. **Cookie-banner preferences.** Seven toggles worded as double negatives, with ON shown grey and OFF shown green.
4. **Certainty slider.** You must be exactly 100% sure. Dragging past 95% bounces you back down; arrow keys work.
5. **Error 418.** "Cancellation is a teapot." Contact Support is a hold-music trap; the small "retry" link is the way forward.
6. **Brenda the chatbot.** Hears every "cancel" as "upgrade".
7. **Typed confirmation.** "I choose to be bored forever", with paste blocked and autocorrect changing *bored* to *board*.
8. **Runaway button.** Cancel dodges the cursor 12 times, then gets tired.
9. **Retention maze.** Touch a wall and you're offered Platinum and sent back to the start.
10. **Mittens the cat.** Pet the cat goodbye 10 times.
11. **Fake loading.** "Processing your heartbreak…"
12. **Victory (sort of).** You're cancelled, with a scorecard of time spent, clicks, offers declined and walls hit. The fine print says it takes effect in 30 days, and Mittens walks off-screen with a suitcase.

## Always-on hostility
- Mittens, an animated cat, follows your cursor with her eyes on every screen, and texts you phone-style notifications ("where r u going").
- A whispering voice reads the guilt lines aloud (there's a SOUND toggle).
- Anything that leads toward cancelling summons a laughing animated devil, a red flash, a low "dun-dun" sound, a whispered guilt line, and a shocked cat.
- A red heartbeat vignette grows stronger the closer you get to leaving.
- The window tilts more on every screen, headlines glitch, and pop-up ads have a close button that dodges.
- The browser tab title rotates through guilt messages.
- 25 seconds of no input: "Session expired for your security", and you're back at screen 1.

## Usability rules broken on purpose
Fitts's law, Hick's law, Jakob's law, Nielsen's heuristics (consistency, visibility of system status, user control, error prevention, recognition over recall), Norman's signifiers, Gestalt proximity, the Doherty threshold, the peak-end rule, sunk cost, and WCAG contrast.

*MegaStream is fictional. No real accounts, payments, personal data or cats.*

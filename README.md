# GainTrain Fitness: The Cancel Screen (Boss Fight)

Keytos Hack · Mini-challenge 01 · *Worst Cancel Screen Ever*

Try cancelling your membership at **GainTrain Fitness**, a made-up gym with the most user-hostile cancellation flow I could design. Gyms are famous for being impossible to quit, so this one leans in. Every screen breaks a real usability rule on purpose. It is hostile, not broken: a determined member can always cancel.

**Play:** open `index.html` in a browser. No build step. Turn your sound on. Press **J** to turn off the session-timeout reset (for judges).

## The boss fight
It opens with a boss-encounter title screen: a laughing devil in flames, *"Level 99 Membership Retention Coach. Nobody has ever cancelled."* and PRESS START. The cancel screen has an HP bar, and each screen you survive knocks one bar off.

1. **Lights off + confirmshaming.** The gym is pitch black except for a flashlight around your cursor. Only the guilt text ("You came 3 times. We believed in you anyway.") and the glowing KEEP button are lit. The exit, the pre-ticked "3 years of Platinum Plus personal training" box (it re-ticks itself) and the fake countdown are hidden in the dark. The headline rewrites itself every time you go for the exit, and KEEP grows.
2. **Retention offers + Memory Lane hostage.** Every cancellation reason leads to an offer ("I'm moving to another city": your membership works at both of our locations, both in this city). Decline three, and you're dropped into a forced slideshow of your "memories" with the gym. Each slide locks for 3 seconds ("Feel it…"), and **Skip rewinds to the first memory**.
3. **Cookie-banner preferences.** Seven toggles worded as double negatives ("Freeze instead of cancel", "Auto-renew me every January 1st"), with ON shown grey and OFF shown green.
4. **Lift to confirm.** Hold the button (or Space) to lift a barbell. Right before the top, Coach Chad shouts "PROGRESSIVE OVERLOAD!" and adds a plate. Twice.
5. **Cancel in person.** "Cancellations must be done in person, with two forms of ID and a doctor's note." The front desk is open Tuesdays, 3:00–3:02 AM. The small "send a certified letter" link is the way forward.
6. **Brenda, Membership Advisor.** A chatbot that hears every "cancel" as "upgrade" ("Did you mean: can-CARDIO?").
7. **Typed confirmation + the Hydra Invoice.** Type "I am okay staying out of shape forever" (paste blocked; autocorrect changes *shape* to **shame**). Then settle your itemized **guilt invoice** ("Coach Chad's emotional damages: $2,900"). You must dispute every line, but **each dispute adds a $19.99 dispute-processing fee** that you also have to dispute.
8. **Runaway button.** Cancel dodges the cursor 12 times, then needs a protein bar.
9. **The treadmill maze.** Walk into the squat rack and you're offered a personal trainer and sent back to the start.
10. **Coach Chad + Shy Buttons + the Future Forecast.** High-five Coach Chad goodbye 10 times. Then pick between two identical buttons that **swap labels when you hover**, so the one under your cursor always says KEEP (only keyboard focus shows the truth). Find the real Cancel and you're fast-forwarded through your future without the gym: 💪 → 🥱 → 📺 → 🦥 → a couch potato that swells until it **explodes into a popcorn storm**, followed by a giant glitching **"FATTU!"** (Hindi slang for scaredy-cat).
11. **Fake loading.** "Emptying your locker… Asking the regional manager…"
12. **Victory (sort of).** Your Platinum membership card is fed into a shredder, followed by a scorecard of time spent, clicks, offers declined, squat racks bumped and surprise plates added. The fine print says it takes effect in 30 days or after your next annual maintenance fee, whichever is later, and Coach Chad waves goodbye as he walks off-screen.

## Always-on hostility
- A live **"KCAL BURNED CANCELLING"** meter tracks your mouse distance, clicks and keystrokes. The final scorecard reveals you burned more calories cancelling than at the gym all year.
- Anything that leads toward cancelling summons a laughing animated devil in flames, a red flash, a low "dun-dun" and a guilt line ("Your quads felt that."), and Coach Chad screams "COACH SAW THAT".
- Coach Chad texts you phone-style notifications ("who's gonna spot me now").
- Fake social proof pops up: "Dave tried to cancel 4 minutes ago. He's still here."
- A red heartbeat vignette grows stronger the closer you get to leaving, the window tilts more on every screen, headlines glitch, the tab title begs you to stay, and pop-up ads have a close button that dodges.
- 25 seconds of no input: "Session expired for your security", and you're back at screen 1.

## Usability rules broken on purpose
Fitts's law, Hick's law, Jakob's law, Nielsen's heuristics (consistency, visibility of system status, user control, error prevention, recognition over recall), Norman's signifiers, Gestalt proximity, the Doherty threshold, the peak-end rule, sunk cost, fake social proof, and WCAG contrast. New patterns invented for this: Memory Lane hostage, the Hydra Invoice, Shy Buttons, and the Future Forecast.
